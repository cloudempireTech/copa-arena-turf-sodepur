The Story Behind Sodepur's Sky Arena

Situated directly at Sodepur Traffic More on the 6th floor of Onex Building, Copa Arena was founded with one clear ambition: to give Kolkata's passionate football and cricket players a world-class arena without having to travel across the city.

By elevating the pitch to the rooftop, players enjoy fresh, cool breezes, zero street congestion, and panoramic views of the city under intense stadium-grade floodlighting.
